Q1 narrative clarity: Can a viewer follow what happens and why? | 1 = The viewer cannot tell what happens or why. | 3 = The main events are clear, but one link or motive is missing. | 5 = Every event and its reason follow in order without confusion.
Q2 emotional communication: Is the stated feeling conveyed without invention? | 1 = The feeling is missing, or the pictures contradict it. | 3 = The feeling is visible but only partly shown, or a little is invented. | 5 = The feeling is shown plainly and nothing beyond the source is added.
Q3 visual storytelling: Do the pictures carry the scene, not only the narration? | 1 = The pictures only repeat the narration or leave out what the story needs. | 3 = Some beats are carried by the pictures and some only by the narration. | 5 = The pictures alone show the main action, the reaction and the place.
Q4 shot progression: Does the order of framings and transitions clarify events, reactions, reveals and space? | 1 = Framings and transitions confuse who is where or what changed. | 3 = The order is mostly clear, but one reaction or space change is unclear. | 5 = Each framing and transition makes the next event, reaction or space easy to read.
Q5 canon fidelity: Is there nothing beyond the ovis and PACKET, and is every claim sourced? | 1 = Several claims are unsourced or invented. | 3 = Claims are sourced, but one detail is loose or unsourced. | 5 = Every claim traces to an ovi or PACKET and nothing is added.
Q6 production feasibility: Can it be built with the registered presets and flat final images? | 1 = It needs new presets or images the registry does not have. | 3 = It is buildable with a few new images or a close substitute. | 5 = It is buildable now with registered presets and flat final images.
